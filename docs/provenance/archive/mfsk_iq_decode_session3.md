# Direct MFSK IQ Decoder — Session 3 Handoff

**Completed:** 2026-07-27  
**Scope:** controlled MFSK32 text vertical slice

## Result

Session 3 decodes the pinned `mfsk32-text-printable` analytic SigMF recording
from IQ through final octets. The decoder automatically identifies the active
region, estimates its 1500 Hz center, searches symbol phase, evaluates all 16
orthogonal tones with matched correlators, selects orientation, converts tone
metrics to coded-bit LLRs, soft-deinterleaves them, applies a 64-state
soft-input Viterbi decoder, and parses MFSK Varicode with the `001` look-ahead
rule.

The exact recovered payload is:

```text
RG-MFSK32 AaZz 09 .,!?/+-=: END
```

The manifest now includes a signal-inferred MFSK32 mode segment, ordered octet
events, STX/EOT/NUL/CR roles, wire intervals, recognition coordinates,
uncalibrated confidence semantics, damage flags, decoded-bit provenance, and a
convenience octet/text summary. Summary diagnostics retain tone, coded-bit,
FEC, and Varicode measurements. `events` and `full` trace levels additionally
retain all tone metric rows, deinterleaved LLR groups, and decoded bits in the
manifest. No persistent IQ or evidence intermediate is written.

## Decisions and limitations

- The reference slice uses original-rate NumPy matched correlators. It does not
  add a resampling or compiled DSP dependency.
- Symbol acquisition searches tone concentration and reports a 48-input-sample
  phase uncertainty. This is adequate for the clean controlled recording and
  remains deliberately conservative.
- Orientation is selected by framing and Varicode plausibility rather than
  assumed from the fixture.
- The current vertical slice requires an integer MFSK32 samples-per-symbol
  ratio. General clock-rate error and non-integer rates are Session 4 work.
- The carrier estimator is a clean-audio occupied-band baseline. Adjacent
  stations, RF drift, reverse orientation, fades, and clock/carrier coupling
  have not yet been accepted on received IQ.
- Viterbi decoding currently starts from the known-zero state and performs
  full-window traceback. Unknown-state and bounded traceback hypotheses remain
  required for mid-transmission cuts and future streaming.
- Invalid Varicode events during transmitter leading fill and its defined idle
  sequence remain explicit evidence; they are not silently removed. The
  convenience payload is strictly the non-control octets between STX and EOT.
- Detailed trace arrays currently live in JSON at `events`/`full` levels. A
  chunked NPZ trace artifact is still preferable before traces become large.
